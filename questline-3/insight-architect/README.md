# Insight Architect

## Dataset
Students Performance in Exams dataset from Kaggle.

## Visualizations

The following visualizations were created using Python and Pandas:

1. **Histogram** – Distribution of math scores.
2. **Bar Chart** – Average math score by gender.
3. **Box Plot** – Math scores based on test preparation course.
4. **Scatter Plot** – Relationship between math and reading scores.
5. **Heatmap** – Correlation between math, reading, and writing scores.

## Key Findings

- Most math scores are concentrated around the middle-to-upper range.
- Average math scores between genders are relatively close.
- Students who completed test preparation generally have higher math scores.
- Math and reading scores show a positive relationship.
- Math, reading, and writing scores are positively correlated.
- Reading and writing scores show a particularly strong relationship.
- The box plot shows differences in the distribution of math scores between students who completed and did not complete test preparation.
